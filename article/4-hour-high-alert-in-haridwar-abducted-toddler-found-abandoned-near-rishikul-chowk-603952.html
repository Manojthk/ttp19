4-Hour High Alert in Haridwar: Abducted Toddler Found Abandoned Near Rishikul Chowk

A quiet night in Haridwar turned tense after a two-year-old girl was allegedly abducted from a family sleeping on the footpath in the Tulsi Chowk area. The incident reportedly took place between 1 am and 2 am.

A quiet night in Haridwar turned tense after a two-year-old girl was allegedly abducted from a family sleeping on the footpath in the Tulsi Chowk area.

The incident reportedly took place between 1 am and 2 am. According to eyewitnesses, the family was asleep on the sidewalk when an unidentified man allegedly took advantage of the darkness and abducted the child. The mother discovered the girl missing after waking up, triggering panic and a frantic search. When the family failed to trace her, police were informed.

Massive Police Response

Following the alert, Senior Superintendent of Police Navneet Singh took charge and mobilised multiple teams, including the local police station, Anti-Human Trafficking Unit (AHTU) and CIU. Checking operations were launched across key junctions and exit routes in the city.

Search operations extended from Tulsi Chowk to the railway station, bus stand and highway areas. Police scanned footage from hundreds of CCTV cameras and deployed drones and mobile surveillance units to track suspicious movement.

Child Found After 4 Hours

After nearly four hours of high alert, the accused allegedly abandoned the girl near Rishikul Chowk and fled under the cover of darkness. Around 5 am, police received information about a child found crying alone. The team reached the spot, confirmed her identity and safely reunited her with her family.

The girl is reported to be safe. A medical examination was conducted as a precaution.

Investigation Underway

While the family expressed relief, the incident has raised serious security concerns. Police are analysing CCTV footage and questioning local residents and those living nearby to identify the suspect. Authorities are probing whether the act was linked to human trafficking or committed by an individual acting alone.

SSP Navneet Singh said a special team has been formed to trace the accused and urged residents not to spread rumours but to report any suspicious activity immediately.

The incident has sparked debate over the safety of families living on footpaths and the need for strengthened night shelter facilities and surveillance in the city.
